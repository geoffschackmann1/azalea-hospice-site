About us

We are Azalea. We are Tyler. We are here for Smith County.

We opened our doors in 2026. We are new. And we think that is a good thing — because we built this hospice with one thing in mind: the families of Smith County.

Azalea Hospice & Palliative Care is locally owned. Not a corporate chain with out-of-state leadership making decisions from a thousand miles away. The people who run this hospice live in East Texas. We send our kids to East Texas schools. We shop at the same grocery stores as the families we serve.

Why "Azalea"? Because East Texas is known for its azaleas — flowers that bloom here year after year, quietly, dependably, beautifully. That is the kind of care we want to provide. The kind you can count on.

Our mission

To accompany people through the last chapter of life with skilled care, attentive presence, and uncompromising dignity — and to support the families who love them, before and after the death.

Our vision

A community where no one dies alone, in unmanaged pain, or without their story being witnessed.

Our values

These six values guide every decision we make, every patient we see, and every phone call we answer.

Presence

We sit with people. We do not rush. We answer the phone at 3 a.m. The visit is not over until the family is steady.

Dignity

Every patient is treated as the full person they are — not a diagnosis, not a Medicare number. Their story is part of their care.

Honesty

We tell the truth, kindly. We do not give false hope or false certainty. Our charts say what happened. Our bills reflect what we did.

Skill

Compassion without competence is not care. We invest in training, peer review, and quality improvement.

Equity

The same quality of care goes to everyone. Charity care is not an exception — it is the rule properly understood.

Stewardship

We are funded by Medicare, Medicaid, insurance, and donors. We treat every dollar as if it were a family member's.

Why Azalea?

Because East Texas deserves a hospice that feels like home. Locally owned. Not a chain. A place where the people making decisions live in the same community as the people receiving care.

We know there are other hospices in Tyler. Some of them are good. But we built Azalea because we believed Smith County deserved another option — one built from scratch around the principle that hospice care should be honest, personal, and always available.

Call us and talk to a real person. Not a sales team, not a call center. A nurse who can answer your questions and help you figure out what comes next. That is who we are.
