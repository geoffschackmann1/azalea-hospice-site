How to choose a hospice

10 questions to ask before you decide. We're happy to answer every one.

As a new hospice in Tyler, we know families have questions about who to trust. You may have heard of Heart to Heart (a corporate chain) or Hospice of East Texas (a well-established nonprofit that has served this area for 40 years). Both offer good care — but every hospice is different, and the right fit matters.

The questions below will help you evaluate any hospice provider, including us. Take this list with you. Call each hospice and ask. The way they answer will tell you a lot.

Call us and ask these questions. We are happy to answer them all. We want you to choose the hospice that is right for your family — whether that turns out to be us or someone else.

10 questions to ask when choosing a hospice

Question 1
Who answers the phone at 3 AM?

We do. A real nurse — not an answering service, not a call center. When you call Azalea at any hour, a nurse picks up and can help you right then.

Question 2
How often will a nurse visit?

We come as often as needed. Not a minimum on a schedule. If your loved one needs daily visits for a few days, we do that. Our care plan fits the patient, not a template.

Question 3
Can I keep my own doctor?

Yes. Your doctor stays involved in your care. Our medical director works alongside them, sharing information and coordinating the plan.

Question 4
What happens if symptoms get worse at night?

You call. A nurse answers. We come if needed. No voicemail, no waiting until morning. If your loved one is in pain or distressed at 2 AM, you get help at 2 AM.

Question 5
Do you serve my area?

Yes. We serve Smith County — Tyler and all surrounding communities. If you live in this area, we can come to you.

Question 6
How does your team communicate with my family?

We update you every visit. You will always know what is happening and what to expect. No surprises, no guessing. If something changes, we tell you right away.

Question 7
What bereavement support do you offer?

13 months of support — calls, letters, support groups, and memorial events. All free. Bereavement is for the family, not the patient. We stay with you after the death.

Question 8
Are you locally owned?

Yes. Azalea Hospice is Tyler-born. We are not a corporate chain with out-of-state owners. Every decision that affects your care is made here, not forwarded to a headquarters somewhere else.

Question 9
How quickly can you start?

We can begin care within 24 hours of a referral. If you call today, we can have a nurse at your door tomorrow. Sometimes the same day.

Question 10
What do your patients' families say about you?

Ask us. We will connect you with families who have given permission to share their experience. We do not put quotes on the website — we let you hear it directly.

The best way to choose a hospice is to call and talk. You will learn more from a 10-minute conversation than from any website. Call us and ask the hard questions. We respect you for doing your research.
