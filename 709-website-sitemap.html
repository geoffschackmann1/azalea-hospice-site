2Content principles

  • One job per page. If a page has two CTAs, it has zero.
  • Sentence case headlines. Plain copy, sixth-grade reading level, EN/ES throughout.
  • Real photographs only. No stock. If we don't have a photo, we use type.
  • The phone number is everywhere. Header, footer, every page, sticky on mobile.
  • No pop-ups, no chat bots. Visiting our website should feel like walking into a quiet office, not a strip mall.
  • Page weight under 350 KB. Many of our visitors are reading on a hospital wifi at 2 AM. Speed is care.

4Editorial calendar

  • Cadence: 2 long-form articles/month, 1 family story/quarter (with consent), 1 staff Q&A/quarter.
  • Owners: Director of Brand drafts; Medical Director clinically reviews everything patient-facing; Compliance reviews anything that touches eligibility, benefits, or claims.
  • The "no" list: no celebrity-death timeliness pieces, no listicles, no AI-generated copy. Every word is written by a human who has been in a patient's home.

5Privacy, accessibility, compliance

  • WCAG 2.1 AA. Tested quarterly. Real-keyboard testing, not just an auditor.
  • No third-party trackers on patient-facing pages other than first-party analytics.
  • Bilingual (EN/ES) site-wide; not a translate-widget bolt-on.
  • HIPAA: no patient PHI in forms or content; the referral form is a TLS-only secure intake routed to EHR, not email.

"Our website's job is to be the first kind voice in a frightening hour. If we get that right, every other metric takes care of itself."
