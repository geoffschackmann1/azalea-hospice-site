The fastest way to reach us is to call. You will talk to a nurse who can answer your questions right away. No menus, no hold, no voicemail.

We answer the phone 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. There is no "office hours" for hospice calls. When you need us, we pick up. Nights, weekends, holidays — it does not matter. A real voice on the other end.

You can also text us at the same number — (903) 123-4567. If you prefer to type instead of talk, we understand. We'll text you back.

Call a nurse 24/7. Do not wait until office hours. If you are worried about a symptom, if something changed, if you just have a question — call. That is what we are here for.
