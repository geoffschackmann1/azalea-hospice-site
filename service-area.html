Service area

We come to you — anywhere in Smith County, Texas.

Azalea Hospice proudly serves Smith County, Texas, and the communities around it. We are based in Tyler, but we come to you anywhere in the county. Your home, an assisted living facility, a nursing home — wherever you call home, our team travels to you.

If you live in Tyler or Whitehouse, you are right in our backyard. Our nurses can be at your door within minutes. If you are out in Lindale or Bullard, we see you just as often. We serve Jacksonville to the south, Troup to the southeast, and Overton to the west.

Smaller communities matter to us too. We serve Flint, Chandler, Brownsboro, Arp, New Chapel Hill, Winona, Hideaway, and Noonday. These towns make up the fabric of Smith County, and we consider it a privilege to care for the people who live in them.

Don't see your area? Call us anyway. We may be able to help. If you live in Smith County or a neighboring community, pick up the phone and ask. The worst that can happen is we tell you the honest answer — but more often than not, we can work something out.

Where we are based

Our administrative office is at 307 S McDonald, Suite 100C, McKinney, TX 75069. But our nurses and aides are on the road every day, traveling to patients all over Smith County. We do not expect you to come to us. We come to you.

Smith County — a quick overview

Smith County sits in northeast Texas, about 90 miles east of Dallas. Tyler is the county seat and the largest city in the region. The county covers roughly 950 square miles, with a mix of urban areas, small towns, and rural communities. Our team covers all of it. Whether you live on a quiet street in Tyler or on a farm outside Brownsboro, we will find you.

From Tyler (our hub), it is roughly 10 minutes to Whitehouse or Flint, 15 minutes to Lindale or Chapel Hill, 20 minutes to Bullard or Troup, and about 25 minutes to Jacksonville or Overton. Chandler, Brownsboro, Arp, and Winona are all within a 20-to-30-minute drive. Hideaway and Noonday are right next door. Every community in Smith County is within reach.
