When is it time for hospice?

If you're asking the question, it's probably time to talk.

It is natural to wonder if it is "too early" or "too late" to call hospice. Most families wrestle with this question for weeks before they reach out. If you are reading this, you are already doing the right thing — looking for answers.

Call us before you think you need to. Most families wish they had. You do not need a doctor's referral to call. You can pick up the phone and talk to a nurse right now, with no obligation.

A simple question to ask yourself

Doctors often use something called the "surprise question." It goes like this:

Would you be surprised if this person died in the next year?

If the honest answer is no, it is worth having a conversation about hospice. A "no" does not mean death is coming tomorrow. It means the illness has reached a point where comfort-focused care might be a better fit than more treatments.

Signs that someone may need hospice

Every illness looks different. But there are some common signs that hospice might be the right choice:

Frequent hospital or ER visits

Repeated trips to the hospital for the same condition, with shorter gaps between stays.

Weight loss

Noticeable weight loss, even when the person is still eating. The body is using more energy than it can take in.

Sleeping more than awake

Spending most of the day sleeping or drowsy. Less energy for conversation or activities.

Trouble swallowing

Difficulty swallowing food, pills, or liquids. This can lead to choking, coughing, or weight loss.

Recurrent infections

Pneumonia, urinary tract infections, or other infections that keep coming back despite treatment.

Declining function

Needing more help with walking, bathing, dressing, or using the bathroom than a few months ago.

You don't need a referral to call us

A lot of people think they need a doctor to tell them it is time, or that a doctor has to refer them to hospice. That is not true. You can call us directly. We will listen to your situation, answer your questions, and — if you want — help you talk with your doctor about whether hospice makes sense.

If you are wondering, call. A conversation is not a commitment. We will never pressure you. We are here to help you understand your options so you can make the choice that is right for your family.
