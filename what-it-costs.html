No copay. No deductible. No hidden fees. No fine print. Hospice is one of the most comprehensive benefits in American healthcare — and it is fully covered.

We understand the number one question families have: "How much does hospice cost?" The answer is simple and honest: for the patient, it costs nothing. Here is how it works.

Who pays for hospice

Medicare Part A

Covers hospice completely. No copay, no deductible. This is the most common way hospice is paid for.

Medicaid (Texas)

Covers hospice in Texas with the same comprehensive benefit as Medicare. All services at no cost.

Private insurance

Most private insurance plans include a hospice benefit. We verify your coverage for you at no cost.

Hospice is the only Medicare benefit that covers not just medical care, but medication, equipment, supplies, and emotional and spiritual support — all at no cost to the patient.

What hospice covers

When a patient chooses hospice, everything related to the hospice diagnosis is covered. That includes:

  • Nursing care — a nurse visits regularly and is on call 24 hours a day, 7 days a week
  • Doctor visits — the hospice medical director oversees the plan of care, working alongside the patient's own doctor
  • Medication — all medications related to the hospice diagnosis, including pain and symptom management
  • Medical equipment — hospital beds, oxygen, walkers, wheelchairs, and other needed equipment
  • Medical supplies — bandages, gloves, incontinence supplies, and more
  • Home health aide services — help with bathing, dressing, and personal care
  • Chaplain services — spiritual care, whatever the patient's faith tradition
  • Social work — help with advance directives, caregiver resources, and emotional support
  • Bereavement support — for the family for 13 months after the death, at no cost

What hospice does NOT cover

There are a few things hospice does not cover. We want you to know them up front — no surprises.

  • Curative treatment for the terminal illness — hospice is for comfort care, not cure. If you choose hospice, you are choosing comfort over aggressive treatment for the terminal diagnosis
  • Room and board at a facility — if you live in a nursing home or assisted living, hospice covers the hospice services but not the cost of the room or daily care at the facility

That is it. Those two things. Everything else related to the hospice diagnosis is covered. There is no fine print. No hidden fees. No surprise bills.

If you have questions about your specific insurance, call us. We will check your coverage at no cost, with no obligation. We will tell you exactly what is covered and what is not — honestly and clearly.

What about the family's expenses?

Families sometimes worry about the cost of medication, supplies, or equipment that hospice covers. The answer is: if it is related to the hospice diagnosis, it is included. You do not pay for it. If something is not covered, we will tell you before it is ordered, and we will help you figure out what to do.

We do not send bills to families for hospice services. We do not surprise you with charges you did not expect. The financial part of hospice is simple: if you qualify for hospice care, the services are covered.
