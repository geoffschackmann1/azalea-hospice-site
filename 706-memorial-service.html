Twice yearly · Spring & Autumn · Open to all bereaved families

A Service of Remembrance.

Each spring and autumn we hold a non-denominational memorial service for the families of every patient we have cared for in the prior six months. Attendance is free, open to all faiths and no faith, and includes a meal afterward. The service is built around three things: each name spoken aloud, a candle lit in their memory, and time for the families to be in a room together.

1Logistics

  • Venue. First Methodist Church, McKinney, fellowship hall (capacity 220). Generously offered at no charge; donation made to their food pantry in lieu.
  • Date & time. Second Sunday of May; second Sunday of November. 3:00 PM, with reception until 5:00 PM.
  • Invitations. Mailed 4 weeks in advance to every family on the bereavement caseload. RSVPs by phone or web. Walk-ins welcome.
  • Translation. Bilingual program (EN/ES). ASL interpreter on request with 7 days' notice.
  • Accessibility. Step-free entry. Reserved front-row seating for mobility devices. Quiet room available for anyone who needs to step out.
  • Children. Welcome. A small children's table with paper, crayons, and a "remembering" coloring page is set up at the back.

3What we do not do

  • No sermon. No specific religious framing. Chaplain may offer an optional benediction at the end of the reception for any family that asks.
  • No fundraising appeal during the service. A small donation card is available in the program; we do not pass a basket.
  • No photography during the reading of names. A volunteer photographer captures candid reception images only with consent.
  • No press, no marketing collateral on tables. This day is for the families.
