We are not accepting patients or referrals right now. If you or someone you love needs hospice care, please don’t wait on us. Here is where to turn.

In an emergency, call 911

If someone is in immediate danger or in crisis, call 911. If someone is already on hospice with another agency, call that agency’s 24-hour line first.

Find a hospice near you

Medicare’s Care Compare lists Medicare-certified hospices by ZIP code. You can also ask your doctor, or call a hospice directly. You don’t need a referral to ask.

Not sure where to start? Dial 211

211 is the Texas information and referral line. Someone there can point you to hospice, home health, and caregiver support in your area.
