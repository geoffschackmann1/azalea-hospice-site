·Mission

To accompany people through the last chapter of life with skilled care, attentive presence, and uncompromising dignity — and to support the families who love them, before and after the death.

·Vision

A community where no one dies alone, in unmanaged pain, or without their story being witnessed.

·Values

ValueWhat it means in practice
PresenceWe sit with people. We do not rush. We answer the phone at 3 a.m. The visit is not over until the family is steady.
DignityEvery patient is treated as the full person they are — not a diagnosis, not a Medicare number, not a difficult family. Their story is part of their care.
HonestyWe tell the truth, kindly. We do not give false hope or false certainty. Our charts say what happened. Our bills reflect what we did.
SkillCompassion without competence is not care. We invest in training, peer review, and quality improvement. We learn from every error.
EquityThe same quality of care goes to the rancher, the recent arrival, the prison patient, the wealthy donor's mother. Charity care is not an exception; it is the rule properly understood.
StewardshipWe are funded by Medicare, Medicaid, insurance, and donors. We treat their money as if it were a family member's. Operating margin exists to extend the mission, not the other way around.

·Promises we make to patients and families

  • You will not be a burden to us. You will be the work.
  • We will tell you what we see, in words you can use.
  • We will help you make the decisions that are yours to make, and we will not make them for you.
  • We will manage symptoms aggressively and conservatively, in that order — comfort first, sedation only when it is the only path to comfort.
  • We will not abandon you. The relationship continues, in another form, through bereavement.
  • We will not bill you for what hospice covers. We will help you understand what we cannot.
  • We will treat your home with the respect we would treat our own.

·Promises we make to staff

  • We will not ask you to do this work alone.
  • We will pay fairly and on time, and explain how we set wages.
  • We will tell you the truth about your performance, in real time.
  • We will not retaliate when you raise a concern in good faith.
  • We will give you tools, training, and time to do this well.
  • We will protect you from unsafe environments — clinically, physically, and emotionally.
  • When the work breaks your heart, we will help you tend to it.

·Promises we make to the community

  • We will not turn anyone away for inability to pay. Charity care is funded.
  • We will be transparent — financially, clinically, and operationally — to the extent regulation and dignity allow.
  • We will partner with the agencies, faith communities, and providers around us, not compete unnecessarily.
  • We will tell the truth about death, in public, where we are invited to.

"Mission is not what we say at meetings. Mission is what is true at 3 a.m., when nobody is watching." — Adopted by the Board of Directors.
